# Version 0.83 development — Audio

> This document records the Version 0.83 development stage. For final Version
> 0.85 behavior, see [v0.85.md](v0.85.md) and [manual-ja.md](../manual-ja.md).

## Hardware

PicoCalc audio uses GP26 (left) and GP27 (right). Both pins share one PWM
slice, so CPB software-mixes audio to stereo PCM and sends it through one
double-buffered DMA/PWM output engine. The internal sample rate is 22050 Hz.

## BASIC

- `BEEP frequency,duration_ms` starts a background tone.
- `PLAY "MML"` starts one background voice.
- `PLAY "MML1","MML2","MML3"` software-mixes up to three voices.
- `PLAY STOP`, `PLAY PAUSE`, `PLAY RESUME`, `PLAY WAIT`.
- `PLAYING()` returns 1 while an audio session is active, including pause.
- `WAVPLAY "FILE.WAV"` starts background streaming from SD.
- `WAVSTOP`, `WAVPAUSE`, `WAVRESUME`.

MML is case-insensitive. Version 0.83 supports notes C-D-E-F-G-A-B, rest R,
sharp #/+, flat -, octaves O0-O8 and </>, default lengths L1/L2/L4/L8/L16/L32,
tempo T, dotted notes, and V0-V15.

## WAV

RIFF/WAVE uncompressed PCM is supported: 8-bit unsigned or 16-bit
little-endian signed; mono or stereo; 11025, 22050, or 44100 Hz. Unknown RIFF
chunks are skipped. Files are streamed through small buffers and are not loaded
whole into RAM.

If `AUTORUN.WAV` exists in the SD root and Startup WAV is enabled, playback
starts in the background during boot. Control Center > Audio sets global volume
(0-100%) and Startup WAV. Defaults are 70% and ON.

WAV playback holds a firmware SD lease. USB storage stops playback before
ownership transfer. A missing/removed SD card, invalid WAV, or non-firmware SD
ownership stops or rejects playback without stopping BASIC.

## Hardware verification

- speakers and headphone jack
- one-voice and three-voice PLAY
- BASIC loop continues during PLAY/WAV
- BREAK exits PLAY WAIT
- mono and stereo WAV
- AUTORUN.WAV and volume
- CPU 150 MHz and 75 MHz
